Persian keyboard (standard layout). Small letters show the QWERTY key (left) and the transliteration (top right); click a key to insert it.
Pick a shape family (letters that differ only by dots), then pick the exact letter. Bottom left shows the Persian keyboard key, top right the transliteration.

Characters without a mapping, including Latin vowels other than a, pass through unchanged. In Latin input, ' gives ء and ` gives ع.
